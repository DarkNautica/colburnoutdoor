# Colburn Outdoor: Brand, Market, and Website Audit

Updated: August 2026

## Executive decision

Colburn Outdoor should be positioned as a **repair-first sprinkler service for existing systems**, with lawn maintenance, exterior upkeep, and restoration as a secondary service. The highest-value message is not “we do outdoor work.” It is:

> Sprinklers fixed. Property restored.

The business should lead with system diagnosis, repair, coverage correction, seasonal startup, in-season checks, and winterization. It should not market new sprinkler installation, lawn installation, landscape construction, or treatment programs.

The geographic strategy is **North Oakland County first**, with targeted service in Troy, Rochester Hills, Rochester, and nearby addresses that fit an efficient route. The website should communicate flexibility without encouraging long-distance, one-off work.

## Market reality

Oakland County is a large, property-rich market: the U.S. Census Bureau reports about 530,460 households, a 72.7% owner-occupied housing rate, and a $343,600 median owner-occupied home value for 2020–2024. Rochester Hills is especially attractive, with a 76.7% owner-occupied rate, a $409,800 median owner-occupied home value, and nearly 29,500 households. Those figures support a service model focused on protecting existing home systems and reducing expensive property problems.

- [U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts: Oakland County](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/oaklandcountymichigan/PST045225)

Local water rules also create a legitimate reason for controller programming and efficiency checks. Troy states that municipal-water sprinkler systems should operate during non-peak hours of 11 p.m. to 5 a.m. This makes timing, controller, overspray, and coverage work more than cosmetic.

## Competitive audit

The market already contains companies selling installation, repair, startup, winterization, and maintenance. H2FLO explicitly targets Troy, Rochester, and Rochester Hills and promotes efficient water distribution, zone configuration, repair, controller upgrades, startup, and winterization. Elite publishes long, city-specific Rochester Hills content and sells repairs, seasonal services, water audits, and maintenance plans.

- [H2FLO Oakland County sprinkler services](https://www.h2flosystems.com/sprinkler-systems-oakland-county)
- [Elite Sprinkler Systems: Rochester Hills](https://elitesprinklersystems.com/service-areas/rochester-hills/)
- [Ryan’s Irrigation](https://www.ryansirrigationllc.com/)

Important conclusion: **“No new installs” is useful scope clarity, but it is not a unique differentiator.** At least one Northern Oakland competitor also focuses on existing systems. Colburn needs to win on a sharper combination:

1. Existing-system specialization.
2. Symptom-led diagnosis instead of generic service lists.
3. A simple seasonal service cycle.
4. North Oakland route focus.
5. Direct phone access for homes, rentals, and small commercial properties.
6. Secondary property upkeep that can be bundled with local sprinkler work.

## Brand audit

### What works

- The house, leaf, and grass mark clearly signals outdoor property service.
- Green remains an appropriate category color.
- “Colburn” is distinctive enough to be the primary verbal asset.
- The new deep-green, acid-lime, cream, and white palette feels more premium and specific than a generic lawn-care template.

### What needed correction

- The old raster logo was cropped in the header and looked soft at desktop size.
- The subtitle “Outdoor Maintenance” was visually cramped and did not make sprinkler service the lead category.
- The logo mark uses gradients and several small details that will not reproduce cleanly on trucks, work shirts, invoices, or small mobile screens.

### Implemented brand direction

The site now uses the existing mark as a recognizable brand asset, paired with a clean text lockup:

**COLBURN OUTDOOR**  
*SPRINKLER + PROPERTY SERVICE*

This is an immediate, low-risk refresh. A future logo project should create a true vector master, flatten the gradients, simplify the roof/leaf/grass construction, and produce horizontal, stacked, one-color, and icon-only versions. The mark should not be redrawn casually in website code.

## Website audit findings

### High priority, fixed

- Sprinkler repair is now the first and dominant offer.
- New-system installation has been explicitly removed from the offer.
- Lawn work is described only as maintenance, upkeep, cleanup, and restoration.
- The hero now uses sprinkler-specific imagery instead of generic lawn-care positioning.
- The cropped header logo has been replaced with a cleaner lockup.
- The call-to-price action stays visible in a sticky desktop header and a fixed mobile call bar.
- The blank/fake-looking coverage map has been removed. It is replaced by a clear route board that communicates North Oakland priority, core cities, and nearby-route flexibility without pretending to show exact geography.
- Common customer symptoms are surfaced near the sprinkler offer.
- A three-touch seasonal service cycle creates a clearer path to repeat business.
- Homes, rental properties, property managers, and businesses are addressed separately.
- Focus-visible states and reduced-motion support are included.
- The frontend relies on working links and native disclosure elements instead of fragile, unnecessary JavaScript.

### High priority, requires owner input

1. **Replace the 704 phone number with a Michigan-local number.** A North Carolina area code beside North Oakland positioning is the largest remaining trust problem. A 248 or 947 business/tracking number would materially improve local credibility. Do not change the public number until calls can be answered reliably.
2. **Confirm insurance and business credentials.** If the business is insured, add the exact, supportable wording. Do not publish a badge or claim without proof.
3. **Update or rebuild the Google Business Profile for the real service area.** Google requires service-area businesses to represent their real-world location and accurate service area. The website, profile, phone, and directories should agree.
4. **Replace the current Google review destination if it belongs to the prior North Carolina market.** Do not send Michigan customers to an old or mismatched listing.
5. **Collect real work photos.** Five to ten strong phone photos of repairs, controller work, broken heads, clean finished coverage, and property restoration will outperform generic stock imagery.
6. **Collect honest reviews.** Do not add invented ratings, testimonials, customer names, years in business, response-time promises, or “licensed and insured” copy.

## Profit-focused offer structure

### Primary: existing sprinkler systems

- Troubleshooting and diagnosis
- Broken or misaligned head repair
- Leaks and valve issues
- Zones that will not run or shut off
- Controller and schedule troubleshooting
- Low pressure, dry spots, overspray, and coverage correction
- Spring startup
- In-season efficiency check
- Fall winterization

Controller replacement or upgrades, backflow work, system expansion, and adding heads should only be marketed after confirming the exact services Colburn can safely and legally perform.

### Secondary: property maintenance and restoration

- Scheduled mowing and edging
- Practical trimming
- Seasonal cleanup
- Overgrowth restoration
- Exterior property upkeep

Do not market sod, seeding programs, landscape installation, bed design, irrigation installation, pesticide/herbicide programs, or broad “full-service landscaping” unless the business intentionally expands.

## Route and sales strategy

The website should remain flexible, but scheduling should protect route density:

- Treat North Oakland as the primary calendar.
- Cluster Troy, Rochester Hills, and Rochester jobs by route/day where practical.
- Ask for ZIP code or address at the beginning of every call.
- For nearby jobs, confirm whether the address fits an existing route before promising a date.
- For landlords and property managers, ask whether multiple properties can be grouped.
- Offer seasonal scheduling during every successful repair call.
- Use property upkeep as a local cross-sell, not as the headline category.

## Recommended next marketing moves

1. Secure and route a Michigan-local phone number.
2. Align Google Business Profile, website, social profiles, and directories to North Oakland.
3. Add real project photos and verified reviews.
4. Publish focused service pages for sprinkler repair, spring startup, winterization, and coverage/controller problems.
5. Publish city pages only for areas the business genuinely wants to route: North Oakland County, Rochester Hills, Rochester, and Troy first.
6. Track calls by page and city rather than adding a complicated quote calculator.
7. Measure booked jobs, average ticket, repeat seasonal jobs, drive time per job, and source—not just website traffic.

## Success measures

- Calls from the preferred service area
- Call-to-book rate
- Average sprinkler ticket
- Percentage of repair customers who schedule a later seasonal visit
- Rental/business accounts with repeat work
- Average drive time between jobs
- Revenue per route day
- Google Business Profile calls and direction/service-area interactions

## Deployment note

The repository is configured for Cloudflare Pages with a Vite build, Pages Functions, and D1. The production script is:

```bash
npm run cf:deploy
```

The exact live workflow should be one of:

1. Merge the approved GitHub pull request into `main` and let a connected Cloudflare Pages project deploy automatically, or
2. From a trusted local checkout, run `npm install`, authenticate Wrangler, and run `npm run cf:deploy`.

Cloudflare environment variables, D1 bindings, dashboard credentials, lead data, and server-side functions must be preserved. The public site refresh should not delete or expose them.
